A small tool solves one problem without a long setup. Its value often becomes apparent in everyday work.

## Remove repetition

A task you repeat manually is a good candidate for automation. A short script can save more time than a complex system.

## Keep the entry point clear

One command, a clear result, and a short instruction next to the code make a tool easier to use.

Usefulness is measured by how much easier the tool makes your work.
